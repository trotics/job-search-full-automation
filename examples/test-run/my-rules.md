# My rules

Last updated: 2026-09-20

## 1. Target roles

- Roles I want: clinical sales specialist; account executive at a health tech company; sales development representative if the pay clears my floor.
- Seniority: entry to mid.
- Roles that are out even if the title sounds close: implementation or training roles with no sales quota; customer success with no quota; anything that is really a nursing job.
- What a good role looks like, in my words: selling software that makes nurses' work easier, to hospitals, where my bedside years count.

## 2. Place

- Home base: Larkfield, Calder.
- Allowed: Larkfield metro; a territory based out of Larkfield; fully remote.
- Not allowed: a territory covering several states.
- Remote rules: fully remote only if Calder residents can apply.
- Nearby cities allowed only above a pay bar: Wrenford, only if the posted pay reaches $100,000. No posted pay there is a no.
- Relocation: no.

## 3. Pay

- Floor: $85,000 a year.
- What counts toward the floor: total pay at target (base plus commission or bonus).
- How I state pay on forms when text is allowed: "$85,000 or more in total pay, open on the split between base and commission."
- The single number I give when a form needs one: 85000.

## 4. Industries and products

- In: health tech; digital health; health data; health insurance; medical devices, software side only.
- Out: pharmaceutical sales (not for me); staffing agencies (how they treat nurses); medical devices where I would work in the operating room.
- Excluded industries (also saved in the tracker settings): Staffing; Pharmaceuticals.

## 5. Experience

- My years in the work I am targeting: no sales years. Six years of hospital nursing (three medical-surgical, three intensive care), which some sales roles count.
- How far I stretch on required years: apply if they ask for up to two years of sales. More than that only if clinical experience is accepted instead.
- Licenses I hold: Registered Nurse, State of Calder.
- Degrees I hold: Bachelor of Science in Nursing.

## 6. Hard noes

- Part-time.
- Commission only.
- Travel over half the time.
- Any license I would need before starting that I do not hold.
